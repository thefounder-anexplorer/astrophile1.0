1Daily 25-minute routine

Use this every day. The 30-day cards tell you what to practise; this tells you how.

5 min

Warm-up: Shadowing

Listen to 30–60 seconds of natural audio and repeat aloud, copying rhythm and intonation.

  • Listen first, then repeat
  • Copy the melody, not just the words
  • Slow audio is fine at first
10 min

Active recall speaking

Use today’s prompt and answer aloud without reading notes.

  • 1–2 minute answers
  • Don’t stop to fix every mistake
  • Use the sentence frames when you get stuck
5 min

Record & review

Record 1–2 minutes and note exactly three things.

  • One thing you did well
  • One grammar or vocabulary issue
  • One pronunciation issue
  • Write them in an error log
5 min

Re-say & spaced review

Answer the same prompt again, better. Then read yesterday’s error log aloud.

  • Same prompt, improved version
  • Re-say yesterday’s corrected sentences
  • Revisit errors from 3 and 7 days ago

3Conversation Lab

Copy a dialogue, say both roles aloud, then remake it with your own details.

At a café

A: Hi, can I get a flat white, please? B: Sure. Anything else? A: Actually, could I also have a croissant? B: Of course. For here or to go? A: To go, please. How much is that? B: That's eight fifty. A: Great, thanks.

Remake it: change the drink, the food, and the price. Say it again without reading.

Asking for directions

A: Excuse me, I'm looking for the train station. Is it far? B: Not really. Go straight, then turn left at the bank. A: Straight and then left at the bank? B: Exactly. It's about five minutes on foot. A: Thanks a lot. I appreciate it.

Remake it: use a real place near you. Change “train station” to “pharmacy”, “museum”, or “bus stop”.

Workplace small talk

A: How was your weekend? B: Pretty good, thanks. I finally tried that new café. How about you? A: Mine was quiet. I mostly caught up on sleep. B: That sounds nice too. Anyway, are you free for a quick call later? A: Sure, what time works for you? B: Let's say three.

Remake it: replace “new café” with a real weekend activity. Change the meeting time.

Sentence frames to copy

Opening: Hi, can I get…? / Excuse me, I'm looking for… Clarifying: Sorry, do you mean…? / Could you say that again? Buying time: Let me think… / What I mean is… Opinion: The reason is… / I'd say… Closing: Thanks a lot. I appreciate it. / Let's say…

Task: pick 3 frames and use each one in a 1-minute answer aloud.

6Rules that make it work

Recall, not re-read. If you can see the answer, it is not retrieval practice.
Feedback is required. Recall alone can cement mistakes — check your errors each session.
Space it out. Revisit each prompt after 1 day, 1 week, and 3 weeks.
Speak out loud. Silent rehearsal does not train the mouth.
Consistency beats length. 15 focused minutes daily beats 2 hours on Sunday.
