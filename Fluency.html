30-Day English Fluency Plan

10 minutes a day • Speak in chunks, not word by word

How this works

Fluency improves when you practice chunks — groups of 2–3 words said as one sound. Each day, spend 10 minutes on drill practice (repeat, make a phrase, repeat, change something) and real practice (combine chunks, make a mini story, record yourself).

4. Change somethingNoun, verb, person, tense.
5. Combine 2 chunksMake a meaningful sentence.
6. Mini story3–5 sentences. It can be silly.
7. Record & listenOptional but powerful.
