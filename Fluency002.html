30-Day English Fluency Plan

Chunking method • 5 minutes a day (plus a 10-minute version when you have time)

How this works

Don't speak word by word — speak in chunks: 2–3 words said as one sound. A chunk is like a bunch of bananas: one unit, quick to pick up.

Verb chunks = what you do (I tend to, I'm used to, I find it easy to). Adverbial chunks = how / when (at the moment, by and large). Put them together and you build fluent sentences.

The two routines

5-minute quick drill every day

1. Pick 3 verb chunkse.g. I tend to, I'm used to, I find it easy to.
2. Repeat & changeSwap the noun, verb, person or tense.
3. Add adverb chunksPut them in front: "At the moment I tend to…"
4. ExtendAdd because / since / as.
5. Mini story3–5 sentences. Illogical is fine.

10-minute extended routine when you have time

A. Drill practiceRepeat chunk → make a phrase → repeat 3× → change something.
B. Real practiceCombine 2 chunks → meaningful sentence → mini story → record.
