How to use this deck

Every day follows the same four-step loop.

01

Input

Listen to or read something in English — a video, podcast, article, series.

02

Notice & Note

Collect useful phrases, chunks, complex grammar — or a repeated mistake.

03

Use

Say it aloud in your own sentences. Speak for 60 seconds. Repeat without notes.

04

Review

Update your Error Diary. Compare with the original. Drill the correct form.

Templates for the diary, drills and summaries are near the end of this deck.

Structure

Your daily rhythm

Full version · 30–45 min

10 min input · 10 min noticing & noting · 15 min speaking aloud · 5 min error diary review.

Minimum version · 15 min

5 min review · 5 min noticing · 5 min speaking aloud. Never skip the speaking.

  • Speak aloud every single day — even alone.
  • Record yourself 2–3 times per week and listen back.
  • Review your Error Diary before every conversation.
  • Don’t collect words — use them in speech the same day.
Roadmap

Weekly themes

WEEK 1 · DAYS 1–7

Speaking activation

Get talking, build the habit, start your Error Diary and find your baseline.

WEEK 2 · DAYS 8–14

Chunks & natural speech

Stop building word by word. Collect and use multi-word chunks.

WEEK 3 · DAYS 15–21

Accuracy under pressure

Complex grammar in speech, real conversations, longer speaking tasks.

WEEK 4 · DAYS 22–30

Integration & confidence

Upgrade simple language, mock tests, final recording and next plan.

Template

Personal Drills

  1. While reading/listening, notice 3 useful phrases you understand but wouldn’t say.
  2. Write each one down.
  3. Make a personal sentence with each — about your life.
  4. Speak for 60 seconds using all three (notes allowed).
  5. Repeat the 60 seconds without notes, from memory.

Template

Error Diary

Fossilized errors need awareness + drills, not just correction.

MISTAKE            → CORRECT            → WHY
"it depend"        → "it depends"       → 3rd person -s
"I live here 3 yrs"→ "I've lived here…"→ present perfect
"say me the time"  → "tell me the time"→ tell + person

DRILL: "It depends on the weather."
       "It depends on the person."
       "It depends on the price."   ×10 aloud

In real conversations, ask a friend or teacher to wave when they hear a target mistake — don’t stop, don’t correct. Just notice.

Every 7 days

Weekly review questions

  1. Which 3 chunks did I actually use in speaking this week?
  2. Which repeated mistake appeared most often?
  3. What did I avoid saying because I didn’t know how?
  4. Did I speak aloud every day? If not, why?
  5. What is one small change for next week?

Use the daily log in your notebook: date · input · 3 things noticed · speaking done ✓ · errors to review.

Bonus

Two habits that accelerate everything

Extensive reading & listening — for pleasure

20–30 minutes several times a week. It reinforces complex grammar, chunks and correct forms without effort.

Sign up for an exam

B2, C1 or IELTS — just above your level. Nothing motivates like a date in the calendar. Don’t stress: use it as fuel.

Key message

Don’t start learning thousands of new words.

Start activating and speaking out what you already know.

Notice it. Note it. Use it.

Don’t sit on it. Start moving — Day 1 is one tap away.
